Quick Facts

  • 10% of global carbon emissions come from fashion production.
  • 85% of textiles are discarded each year.
  • Fashion is the second-largest industrial consumer of water.
  • Synthetic clothing releases huge quantities of microplastics.
  • Textile dyeing is a major source of water pollution.

Source: Earth.Org’s article on fast fashion’s environmental impact

Artist: Chloe Baines

Baines works with the material rather than against it, allowing its existing character to remain legible.

Faggot Seam

When researching relatively simple ways to stitch fabric together -- because I don't have access to a sewing machine right now -- I came across faggot stitching. This led me to deciding to make embroidery a very visible and central element of the final design.
