Quick Facts

  • 10% of global carbon emissions come from fashion production.
  • 85% of textiles are discarded each year.
  • Fashion is the second-largest industrial consumer of water.
  • Synthetic clothing releases huge quantities of microplastics.
  • Textile dyeing is a major source of water pollution.

Source: Earth.Org’s article on fast fashion’s environmental impact
