Reflection + Evaluation
  • During my presentation, Jonathan asked: “How does the card make you feel?”
  • I couldn’t immediately answer. His question continued to reverberate in my mind all weekend.
  • The irony: in tarot, one’s personal, intuitive response to a card is often more important than any “official” interpretation.
  • Realized I’d approached the entire project almost exclusively intellectually:
    • Researching Roman Britain: podcasts, academic articles, historical sources.
    • Exploring Celtic and Roman design.
    • Connecting historical, symbolic, and conceptual ideas.
  • Nothing wrong with this approach, but I’d completely neglected my own feelings and instincts.
  • A significant realization: for my next few projects, I want to quiet my intellect and work more impulsively, instinctively, and intuitively.
